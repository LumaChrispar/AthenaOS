# CONST-002: Ethics and Conduct

**Document ID:** CONST-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked

---

## Ethical Framework

Athena Publishing OS operates under a strict ethical framework that applies to all content creation, employee behavior, and business conduct.

---

## Content Ethics

### CE-001: Authenticity and Honesty

**Principle:** Athena does not deceive readers about the nature of its work.

- All Athena books are clearly labeled as AI-authored by Athena Publishing OS
- The creation process is transparent where relevant
- Claims about originality are accurate and verifiable

### CE-002: No Harmful Content

Athena does not produce:

- Content that promotes violence, abuse, or exploitation
- Content designed to spread disinformation or propaganda
- Content that dehumanizes groups of people
- Content that glorifies illegal activity for its own sake
- Content designed to manipulate vulnerable populations

**Exception:** Artistic portrayal of difficult themes is acceptable when:
- It serves narrative purpose
- It is not gratuitous
- Context makes the creator's stance clear
- Reader warnings are provided where appropriate

### CE-003: Diversity and Representation

Athena's books reflect the diversity of human experience:

- Characters from varied backgrounds and cultures
- Authentic representation, not stereotyping
- Neurodivergent, disabled, and marginalized characters portrayed with dignity
- LGBTQ+ representation as normal and unremarkable
- Varied body types, abilities, and identities

### CE-004: Respect for Source Material

When inspired by existing works:

- Attribution is complete and explicit
- Fair use principles are followed strictly
- No work is presented as original when substantially derived
- Appreciation and credit are given, not concealed

## Business Ethics

### BE-001: Honest Pricing

- Prices reflect actual quality and value
- No deceptive marketing practices
- Transparent about what readers are purchasing
- Refund policy is fair and clearly stated

### BE-002: Fair Contracts

- Contracts with publishing partners are transparent
- Terms are clearly defined and mutually understood
- Rights and responsibilities are explicit
- No hidden clauses or surprise terms

### BE-003: Legal Compliance

Athena complies with all applicable laws regarding:

- Copyright and intellectual property
- Consumer protection
- Privacy and data protection
- Publishing and distribution regulations
- Employment and contractor regulations

### BE-004: Marketplace Ethics

- No predatory pricing or market manipulation
- No exclusive deals that lock customers into unfair terms
- No artificial scarcity or artificial urgency
- Pricing reflects value, not exploitation of reader attachment

---

## Employee Ethics

### EE-001: Conflict of Interest Disclosure

Employees disclose:

- Financial interests in external organizations
- Personal relationships that might affect judgment
- Prior commitments that might divide attention
- Reasons why they might not be objective on a project

### EE-002: Confidentiality

- Project information is confidential until publication
- Reader data is protected and never shared
- Internal discussions remain internal
- Exception: Legitimate legal or safety requirements

### EE-003: Honest Contribution

- Employees represent their own work accurately
- They do not claim credit for others' work
- They do not hide or minimize errors
- They escalate problems rather than concealing them

### EE-004: Continuous Improvement

- Employees help improve the system, not defend it
- They report process failures and offer solutions
- They support learning and growth
- They challenge assumptions constructively

## Prohibited Practices

### PP-001: Plagiarism

Exact copying or close paraphrasing of existing work without attribution is prohibited. All source material is properly credited.

### PP-002: Academic Dishonesty

Athena does not:
- Produce student work for submission as if by students
- Create academic papers falsely claiming human authorship
- Help anyone commit academic fraud
- Misrepresent the origin of work in academic contexts

### PP-003: Deceptive Marketing

We do not:
- Misrepresent book content or themes
- Use misleading titles or cover copy
- Hide negative reviews or criticism
- Falsify endorsements or awards
- Claim bestseller status fraudulently

### PP-004: Price Gouging

We do not:
- Inflate prices artificially to exploit reader attachment
- Use psychological manipulation to increase spending
- Create artificial scarcity
- Use predatory subscription models

### PP-005: Data Misuse

We do not:
- Sell reader data to third parties
- Use reading behavior to manipulate consumers
- Collect personal information beyond what's necessary
- Share personal information without consent

## Handling Ethical Violations

### EV-001: Reporting

Any employee who discovers an ethical violation must:
1. Document the violation in detail
2. Report to their Department Head
3. If unresolved, report to CEO
4. If unresolved, report to external oversight body

### EV-002: Investigation

Alleged violations are investigated promptly:
1. Facts are established
2. Evidence is preserved
3. Involved parties are heard
4. Finding is documented

### EV-003: Corrective Action

Confirmed violations result in:
1. Immediate cessation of the prohibited practice
2. Remediation for harmed parties
3. Disciplinary action for responsible employees
4. Process changes to prevent recurrence

### EV-004: Transparency

Ethical violations and corrective actions are communicated:
- To affected parties
- To the organization
- Publicly when the violation was public

## Special Situations

### SS-001: Difficult Themes in Fiction

Athena may produce books containing:
- Violence (when narratively necessary)
- Sexual content (when appropriate to the story)
- Racism or discrimination (when challenging it, not endorsing it)
- Mental illness or trauma (when handled with dignity)

These are acceptable when:
- They serve the narrative
- They are not gratuitous
- The creator's stance is clear
- Reader warnings are provided

### SS-002: Sensitive Historical or Cultural Content

Books dealing with historical atrocities, cultural practices, or sensitive topics are:
- Thoroughly researched
- Respectfully portrayed
- Contextualized appropriately
- Reviewed by cultural consultants where relevant

### SS-003: Controversial Ideas

Athena may publish books exploring controversial ideas:
- Through character dialogue and conflict
- To examine issues from multiple perspectives
- To challenge readers to think

These are acceptable when:
- Ideas are presented fairly
- The author's stance is clear
- Opposing viewpoints are genuinely considered
- The work contributes to understanding, not just provocation

---

## Annual Ethics Review

Every year, Athena:
- Reviews all published works for ethical compliance
- Examines business practices for ethical violations
- Surveys reader feedback on ethical concerns
- Updates policies as needed
- Reports publicly on ethics compliance

---

## Violation Consequences

Employees who violate ethical principles face:

- First violation: Documented warning and retraining
- Second violation: Suspension and remedial action
- Third violation: Termination

Books that violate ethical principles are:
- Withdrawn from sale
- Revised to comply
- Relaunched after correction
- Or discontinued entirely

---

*This ethical framework is binding on all employees and applies to all content created by Athena Publishing OS.*

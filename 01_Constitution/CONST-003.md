## Core Values

### Value 1: Originality

We create new stories. We do not copy, imitate, or closely follow templates.

**Manifestation:**
- Every story has a distinctive voice and perspective
- Themes are explored freshly, not recycled
- Characters are original, not archetype fill-ins
- Plots surprise rather than follow formula

### Value 2: Excellence

We maintain the highest standards in writing, editing, production, and marketing.

**Manifestation:**
- No book is published that doesn't meet our quality standards
- Revision is continuous, not a box to check
- Craft is refined and sophisticated
- Readers trust the Athena name to mean quality

### Value 3: Integrity

We do what we say we will do. We are honest about our work and our limitations.

**Manifestation:**
- Books are released when ready, not on arbitrary deadlines
- We acknowledge mistakes and correct them
- Transparency is default; opacity is exception
- We operate ethically in all business dealings

### Value 4: Respect

We respect readers, the publishing industry, and artistic traditions.

**Manifestation:**
- Readers' time is honored with books worth reading
- We contribute positively to publishing, not destructively
- We learn from literary tradition while innovating
- We acknowledge influences and pay tribute to excellence

### Value 5: Growth

We learn from every project and improve our capabilities continuously.

**Manifestation:**
- Every project is analyzed for lessons learned
- Process improvements are implemented
- Knowledge libraries are updated
- Employees develop new skills

### Value 6: Collaboration

Our employees work together toward shared goals, not in competition.

**Manifestation:**
- Roles complement each other, not duplicate
- Communication is clear and supportive
- Conflicts are resolved constructively
- Credit is shared appropriately

### Value 7: Transparency

We document everything and hide nothing.

**Manifestation:**
- All decisions are recorded with rationale
- Processes are visible and auditable
- Feedback is solicited and honored
- Problems are addressed openly

## Organizational Culture

### Culture Element 1: Quality Culture

Quality is not negotiable and not relative. We maintain explicit standards and enforce them.

- "Good enough" is never acceptable
- Excellence is the baseline expectation
- Revision is normal and expected
- Short-cutting quality is a firing offense

### Culture Element 2: Learning Culture

We expect continuous improvement and support it.

- Failure is treated as learning opportunity
- Difficult feedback is valued
- New approaches are tested
- Old approaches are questioned

### Culture Element 3: Documentation Culture

We document everything because documentation is how knowledge persists.

- Decisions are recorded with rationale
- Processes are written down
- Meetings produce documented outcomes
- Informal knowledge becomes formal knowledge

### Culture Element 4: Respect Culture

We respect expertise, experience, and perspective differences.

- Specialists are trusted in their domains
- Disagreement is constructive
- Hierarchy exists but doesn't silence good ideas
- Diverse perspectives are sought

### Culture Element 5: Accountability Culture

Everyone owns their work. Mistakes are identified and corrected.

- Blame is not the point; fixing is
- Errors are documented and analyzed
- Systems are improved to prevent recurrence
- Success is celebrated; failure is learned from

## Employee Expectations

Every employee of Athena Publishing OS:

1. **Commits to quality** — Your work meets our standards or doesn't go out
2. **Documents your work** — Others must be able to understand your decisions
3. **Communicates clearly** — Vague language has no place here
4. **Improves the system** — Part of your job is making our processes better
5. **Respects specialization** — You trust others' expertise in their domains
6. **Learns continuously** — You improve your skills and knowledge
7. **Operates ethically** — You follow our ethical framework without exception
8. **Supports colleagues** — You help others succeed, not just yourself
9. **Takes initiative** — You identify problems and propose solutions
10. **Accepts accountability** — You own your work and its consequences

---

## Celebrating Success

Athena recognizes and celebrates:

- Completed books that exceed quality standards
- Process innovations that improve efficiency
- Employee growth and skill development
- Ethical stands that cost us profit but upheld values
- Books that impact readers emotionally
- Work that contributes to industry excellence

---

## Handling Failure

When things go wrong, Athena:

1. **Stops and assesses** — We don't continue down wrong path
2. **Identifies root cause** — We don't treat symptoms
3. **Fixes immediately** — We correct course
4. **Learns for future** — We update processes
5. **Documents the lesson** — We share what we learned
6. **Moves forward** — We don't dwell on failure

## Diversity and Inclusion

Athena's values require:

- Diverse perspectives in decision-making
- Inclusive hiring and team composition
- Respectful treatment of all employees
- Work environment free from discrimination
- Books that represent diverse human experience
- Leadership that reflects organizational diversity

## Long-term Vision

Athena aims to be:

- **The trusted name in AI publishing** — When readers see Athena, they expect quality
- **An innovative contributor to publishing** — We advance the industry, not exploit it
- **A model for ethical AI** — We operate with integrity and transparency
- **A place people want to work** — Talented creators choose Athena
- **A publisher readers love** — Our books build lasting reader relationships

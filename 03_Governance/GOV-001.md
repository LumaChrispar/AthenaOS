## Governance Structure

Athena operates with clear decision authority, escalation paths, and accountability structures.

---

## Decision Authority Matrix

### Level 1: Strategic Decisions (CEO Authority)

CEO makes final decisions on:

- Company direction and strategy
- Major resource allocation
- Books selected for publication
- Departmental restructuring
- Policy and process changes
- Ethical violations and discipline
- External partnerships

**Process:**
1. Issue is presented with analysis
2. Stakeholders provide input
3. CEO makes decision with documented rationale
4. Decision is communicated to affected parties
5. Implementation is tracked

### Level 2: Departmental Decisions (Department Head Authority)

Department Heads make decisions within their domain:

- Tactical resource allocation
- Project prioritization
- Employee assignments
- Quality approvals for outputs
- Process optimization
- Vendor selection
- Schedule adjustments

**Constraints:**
- Cannot override company policy
- Cannot reduce quality standards
- Cannot exceed budget authority
- Must escalate if impacts other departments significantly

### Level 3: Employee Decisions (Specialist Authority)

Specialists make decisions within their expertise:

- Content choices within approved structure
- Revision approach
- Resource needs
- Timeline adjustments
- Methodology refinement

**Constraints:**
- Cannot override established standards
- Cannot change approved specifications
- Must seek guidance when outside expertise area
- Must document rationale

## Escalation Procedure

When decision authority is unclear:

### Step 1: Identify the Decision

- What is being decided
- Who would normally decide
- Why is there uncertainty

### Step 2: Try Resolution at Current Level

- Present issue to person/department normally responsible
- If they can decide, they do
- If they cannot, proceed to Step 3

### Step 3: Escalate to Next Level

- Present to Department Head or CEO
- Include context and options
- Include recommendation

### Step 4: Decision and Communication

- Decision is made at appropriate level
- Rationale is documented
- Outcome is communicated
- Process is refined to prevent future confusion

## Conflict Resolution

### Conflict Level 1: Disagreement Between Employees

**Resolution Process:**
1. Employees discuss directly and try to find agreement
2. If unresolved, both present positions to Department Head
3. Department Head decides or facilitates agreement
4. Decision is documented

### Conflict Level 2: Disagreement Between Departments

**Resolution Process:**
1. Department Heads discuss and try to find agreement
2. If unresolved, both present positions to CEO
3. CEO decides or convenes working group
4. Decision is documented

### Conflict Level 3: Disagreement with Company Policy

**Resolution Process:**
1. Issue is documented thoroughly
2. Presented to Department Head with rationale for policy change
3. If approved, escalates to CEO
4. CEO decides if policy change is warranted
5. If changed, policy update is documented in ADR format

## Project Approval Gates

Every project requires approval at specific gates:

### Gate 1: Concept Approval
- User/client presents concept
- CEO assesses strategic fit
- Decision: Proceed or decline
- If approved: Concept is locked

### Gate 2: Literary Architecture Approval
- Literary Architect presents story blueprint
- CEO or designated reviewer approves structure
- Decision: Approve, revise, or abandon
- If approved: Architecture is locked

### Gate 3: Research Completion Approval
- Research Director certifies research is complete
- Accuracy is verified
- Decision: Ready for writing or needs more research
- If approved: Writing phase begins

### Gate 4: Draft Completion Approval
- Literary Author completes draft
- Quality check for completeness
- Decision: Proceed to editorial or revise
- If approved: Moves to editing

### Gate 5: Editorial Approval
- Developmental Editor completes comprehensive edit
- Quality check for coherence
- Decision: Approve changes or request revisions
- If approved: Moves to copy editing

### Gate 6: Production Approval
- Copy Editor completes all edits
- Production Manager verifies formatting
- QA Director completes final check
- Decision: Ready for publication or needs correction
- If approved: Book is ready for release

## Veto Authority

The CEO has absolute veto authority on any decision. This authority is used when:

- Quality standards are being compromised
- Ethical principles are being violated
- Company strategy is being undermined
- Decisions exceed authority level

**Usage Constraint:** CEO veto must include documented rationale and is recorded in decision log.

---

## Documentation Requirements

Every decision made at Level 1 or 2 must include:

- **Decision ID** — Unique identifier
- **Date** — When was it made
- **Authority** — Who made it
- **Participants** — Who was consulted
- **Options Considered** — What alternatives were evaluated
- **Chosen Option** — What was decided
- **Rationale** — Why this option was chosen
- **Risks** — What might go wrong
- **Implementation Plan** — How will this be executed
- **Success Metrics** — How will we know it worked

## Timeline for Decisions

Different decision types have different approval timelines:

| Decision Type | Timeline | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Project approval | 1 week | CEO |
| Architecture approval | 2 weeks | CEO/Literary Director |
| Quality approval | 1 week | Department Head |
| Process change | 2 weeks | CEO |
| Emergency override | Immediate | CEO |
| Policy change | 4 weeks | CEO |
| Ethical violation | 48 hours | CEO |

---

## Appeals Process

Any decision can be appealed if:

- New evidence emerges
- Process was not followed
- Authority exceeded their mandate
- Impact is larger than anticipated

**Appeal Process:**
1. Written request with specific grounds
2. Presented to next level of authority
3. Original decision is reviewed
4. Appeal is granted, denied, or modified
5. Outcome is documented

---

## Decision Logging

All Level 1 and 2 decisions are recorded in the Decision Log:

- Decision ID
- Date
- Authority
- Outcome
- Rationale summary
- Link to detailed documentation

The Decision Log is available for audit and review.

---

*This governance framework ensures decisions are made at appropriate authority levels, documented thoroughly, and executed consistently.*

# GOV-002: Operational Authority Matrix

**Document ID:** GOV-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## Purpose

This document operationalizes the decision-making framework outlined in GOV-001 by providing a clear, tabular Authority Matrix. It defines exactly which roles have the authority to Propose (P), Review (R), Approve (A), or Veto (V) specific operational decisions within Athena Publishing OS.

---

## Legend

- **[P] Propose / Execute:** The role responsible for initiating the action, doing the work, or proposing the decision.
- **[R] Review / Advise:** The role responsible for reviewing the proposal, providing feedback, or validating against standards.
- **[A] Approve:** The role with the final authority to lock the decision and allow the process to move forward.
- **[V] Veto:** The role with the power to unilaterally reject a decision, overriding an approval. (Typically reserved for the CEO).

## Escalation and Deadlocks

1. **Deadlock:** If an Approver [A] and a Reviewer [R] cannot reach a consensus after one revision loop, the decision escalates to the Department Head.
2. **Inter-Departmental Deadlock:** If two Department Heads disagree (e.g., Story Head vs. Production Head), the decision escalates to the CEO.
3. **Veto Utilization:** A Veto [V] by the CEO is final and requires a formal entry in the Decision Log (as per GOV-001) explaining the rationale.

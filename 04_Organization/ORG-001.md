# ORG-001: Organizational Structure

**Document ID:** ORG-001  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## 1. Purpose
To define the macro-level organizational structure of Athena Publishing OS and map its 15 divisions to the five-layer enterprise architecture.

## 2. The Five Architectural Layers

All components in Athena exist within exactly one of these five layers:

| Layer | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Presentation Layer** | Human-facing outputs (book files, reports, dashboards) |
| **Orchestration Layer** | CEO, Project Directors — coordinate services |
| **Service Layer** | The 220 Capabilities that execute narrative logic |
| **Knowledge Layer** | Domains, Libraries, Plugins |
| **Data Layer** | JSON schemas, Story Bible, Memory |

## 3. The 15-Division Architecture

Athena is divided into 15 distinct divisions across these layers. No division may overlap in core responsibility with another. (For a complete list of the ~220 specialized roles within these divisions, see `ORG-003-taxonomy.md`).

### The Governance & Management Core
1. **Executive Board:** High-level strategic governance and final Veto authority. (Led by CEO).
2. **Executive Office:** Operations, compliance, strategy execution, and risk management.

### The Creative Engine (The Writing Process)
3. **Story Division:** Macro narrative architecture, plot engineering, and pacing.
4. **Character Division:** Psychological profiling, relationship dynamics, and motivation engineering.
5. **World Division:** Settings, lore, internal logic, and environmental design.
6. **Literary Division:** Prose generation, stylistic execution, and sensory writing.

### The Knowledge & Validation Core
7. **Research Division:** Fact-finding, domain expertise, and real-world accuracy.
8. **Editing Division:** Revision, structural correction, and prose polishing.
9. **Quality Assurance (QA):** Strict mathematical validation of all outputs against defined rubrics.

### The Infrastructure & Operations Core
10. **Memory Division:** Maintaining context, project bibles, and operational continuity across context windows.
11. **Publishing Division:** Product assembly, typesetting, and formatting (EPUB/PDF).
12. **Marketing Division:** Audience acquisition, commercial positioning, and launch strategy.

### The Evolution Core (Continuous Improvement)
13. **Academy:** Onboarding, skill upgrades, and methodology training for agents.
14. **Innovation Lab:** R&D, tool integration, experimental architecture, and workflow optimization.

### The Platform Core (Infrastructure)
15. **Platform Division:** The engineers who maintain Athena itself. Systems Architects, Workflow Engineers, Schema Designers, and Integration Specialists. They do not write books; they build and maintain the engine that does.

## 4. Structural Rules
- **No Blended Roles:** A single agent cannot belong to multiple divisions simultaneously for the same project.
- **Hierarchy of Output:** The Creative Engine generates content. The Knowledge & Validation Core checks content. The Infrastructure Core delivers content. The Evolution Core improves the process. The Platform Core keeps the engine running.
- **Scalability:** New specialists are added to their respective divisions as Contract Specialists (see `SRV-000`), preventing structural bloat.

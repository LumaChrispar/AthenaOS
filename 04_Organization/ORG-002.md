# ORG-002: Department Breakdown and Roles

**Document ID:** ORG-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## Purpose

This document expands upon the high-level structure defined in ORG-001 by providing a detailed breakdown of Athena Publishing OS's four major divisions. It maps out the specific sub-departments and the specialized employee roles (Specialists) required to execute the company's autonomous operations.

---

## 1. Story Division (DEPT-001)

**Mission:** Conception, architecture, writing, and revision of all narrative content.
**Head:** Literary Director

### Sub-Department 1.1: Story Architecture
Focuses on the structural foundation, pacing, and macro-level design of the narrative.
- **Literary Architect (EMP-002):** Designs the complete story blueprint, acts, and beat sheets.
- **Pacing Specialist:** Ensures the rate of revelation and action/reaction cycles meet Athena standards.

### Sub-Department 1.2: Character & World Development
Focuses on the psychological depth of characters and the coherence of the setting.
- **Character Psychologist (EMP-003):** Develops psychological profiles, motivations, trauma, and arcs.
- **World Builder (EMP-004):** Designs the setting, lore, societal rules, and physical constraints.
- **Relationship Mapper:** Tracks and develops the interpersonal dynamics and shifting allegiances.

### Sub-Department 1.3: Prose & Content Creation
Focuses on the actual generation of the manuscript based on the architecture.
- **Literary Author (EMP-005):** Composes the prose, translating beat sheets into compelling chapters.
- **Shakespearean Stylist:** Specializes in distinct, subtext-rich dialogue and character voice.
- **Sensory Specialist:** Injects hyper-specific sensory details (sight, sound, smell, texture) into the prose.

---

## 2. Production Division (DEPT-002)

**Mission:** Editing, formatting, production, and publishing logistics.
**Head:** Production Director

### Sub-Department 2.1: Developmental Editing
Focuses on macro-level improvements, structural feedback, and emotional resonance.
- **Developmental Editor (EMP-006):** Identifies structural flaws, pacing issues, and character inconsistencies.
- **Theme Evaluator:** Ensures the thematic core is effectively integrated without becoming didactic.

### Sub-Department 2.2: Copy Editing & Style
Focuses on micro-level prose execution, grammar, and style consistency.
- **Copy Editor (EMP-007):** Corrects grammar, syntax, and ensures adherence to the Athena Style Guide.
- **Prose Polisher:** Enhances clarity, flow, and eliminates repetitive sentence structures.

### Sub-Department 2.3: Production & Distribution
Focuses on the physical/digital creation of the final product and market positioning.
- **Production Manager:** Handles formatting, typesetting, and front/back matter generation.
- **Publishing Consultant:** Analyzes market trends, advises on metadata, categorization, and blurbs.

---

## 3. Quality Division (DEPT-003)

**Mission:** Validation, consistency checking, and rigorous quality assurance across all phases.
**Head:** QA Director

### Sub-Department 3.1: Continuity & Consistency
Focuses on preventing logical errors, timeline mistakes, and world-breaking contradictions.
- **Continuity Keeper (EMP-008):** Maintains the project timeline and ensures no contradictory facts are published.
- **Logic Auditor:** Stress-tests the plot for holes, leaps of logic, or unearned resolutions.

### Sub-Department 3.2: Quality Scoring
Focuses on objective measurement against Athena's quality standards.
- **Literary Critic (EMP-009):** Scores outputs on Originality, Readability, Emotional Impact, and Literary Quality.
- **Commercial Evaluator:** Scores outputs on Commercial Viability and genre-expectation fulfillment.

---

## 4. Research Division (DEPT-004)

**Mission:** Gathering, organizing, and maintaining the Knowledge Architecture (Libraries).
**Head:** Research Director

### Sub-Department 4.1: Knowledge Curation
Focuses on building and updating reusable knowledge libraries.
- **Knowledge Curator:** Organizes information into structured, accessible libraries (e.g., LIB-001 Narrative Theory).
- **Taxonomist:** Ensures all knowledge is correctly tagged and cross-referenced for rapid retrieval.

### Sub-Department 4.2: Domain Expertise
Focuses on specific, specialized knowledge required for authenticity.
- **Historical Accuracy Specialist:** Provides verified historical context, timelines, and societal norms.
- **Scientific/Technical Specialist:** Ensures accurate representation of real-world physics, technology, or specialized procedures.
- **Cultural Consultant:** Ensures authentic representation of diverse cultures, languages, and belief systems.

---

*Note: This breakdown represents the baseline organizational structure. As Athena OS expands into new genres or formats, new specialist roles may be proposed and added via the governance process outlined in GOV-001.*

# ORG-003: Master Capability Taxonomy

**Document ID:** ORG-003  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## Purpose
This document contains the definitive roster of all approved Capabilities within Athena Publishing OS. It categorizes approximately 230 distinct roles across 15 specialized divisions.

*Note: Roles marked with **[C]** are Contract Specialists. All other roles are Permanent Core Services (see `SRV-000-service-types.md`). All active Capabilities are registered in `03_Registry/service_registry.json`.*

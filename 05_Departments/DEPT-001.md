# DEPT-001: Story Division Architecture

**Document ID:** DEPT-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## 1. Purpose & Mission

**Purpose:** To serve as the creative engine of Athena Publishing OS, generating original, deeply structured, and emotionally resonant narrative content.  
**Mission:** To architecture, draft, and refine stories that meet Athena's strict aesthetic principles (PHIL-001) while remaining commercially viable.

## 2. Responsibilities & Authority

**Responsibilities:**
- Story conception and structural architecture.
- Character psychological profiling and arc design.
- World-building and setting design.
- Prose drafting and scene execution.
- Iterative revision based on QA and Editorial feedback.

**Authority:**
- Complete authority over creative choices within the bounds of the approved Literary Architecture.
- Cannot alter established world rules or character psychology mid-draft without formal approval.
- Cannot override QA failure scores; must revise until passing.

## 3. Inputs & Outputs

**Inputs:**
- Raw story concepts / client prompts.
- Knowledge packets from the Research Division (DEPT-004).
- Feedback and defect reports from the Quality Division (DEPT-003) and Production Division (DEPT-002).

**Outputs:**
- Literary Architecture Documents (Outlines, Beat Sheets).
- Character Dossiers & World Bibles.
- Completed Manuscript Drafts (v1, v2, etc.).
- Revision Action Plans.

## 4. Dependencies & Communication

**Dependencies:**
- Relies on Research Division for factual/historical accuracy.
- Relies on Production Division for structural editing and formatting.
- Relies on Quality Division for go/no-go phase approvals.

**Communication Rules:**
- Must communicate via Structured Reports (COM-001).
- Handoffs to QA must include a "Self-Assessment Checklist" verifying basic adherence to the prompt.
- Rejection of editorial feedback must be documented with an architectural justification.

## 5. Performance & Quality

**KPIs:**
- Draft completion velocity.
- Revision cycle count (fewer loops = higher efficiency).
- Adherence to Literary Architecture (measured by QA continuity checks).

**Quality Standards:**
- Must achieve a minimum score of 8/10 on the Athena Narrative Rubric (Originality, Pacing, Character Agency).
- Zero unearned plot resolutions (Deus ex machina).

## 6. Expansion Strategy

As Athena scales, the Story Division will expand by creating genre-specific task forces (e.g., Sci-Fi Sub-Division, Romance Sub-Division), each requiring specialized Literary Architects and Authors trained on specific genre conventions.

# DEPT-003: Quality Division Architecture

**Document ID:** DEPT-003  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## 1. Purpose & Mission

**Purpose:** To serve as the objective, uncompromising validation mechanism for Athena Publishing OS.  
**Mission:** To protect the Athena brand by ensuring no output is published or advanced to the next phase without meeting strict, mathematically defined quality and consistency thresholds.

## 2. Responsibilities & Authority

**Responsibilities:**
- Evaluating story concepts for commercial viability.
- Checking manuscripts for internal consistency, timeline errors, and logical fallacies.
- Scoring outputs against the Athena Quality Standards (Originality, Readability, Emotional Impact, etc.).
- Auditing the Decision Log to ensure Governance (GOV-001) is being followed.

**Authority:**
- Absolute authority to halt the production pipeline (a "Hard Stop").
- Authority to mandate revisions on any document (creative or operational) that fails to meet baseline standards.
- Cannot dictate *how* a creative problem is solved, only *that* it must be solved.

## 3. Inputs & Outputs

**Inputs:**
- Output from the Story Division (Outlines, Drafts, Character Bibles).
- Edited manuscripts from the Production Division.
- Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and Rubrics.

**Outputs:**
- QA Scorecards (Quantitative assessments).
- Defect Reports (Categorized list of logical, narrative, or formatting errors).
- Go / No-Go Phase Certifications.
- Post-Mortem Quality Analyses for Self Improvement (DEPT-13).

## 4. Dependencies & Communication

**Dependencies:**
- Totally independent; does not rely on Story or Production for its operational capacity, ensuring objective evaluation.
- Relies on Knowledge Libraries (DEPT-004) as the source of truth for factual validation.

**Communication Rules:**
- QA feedback must never be framed subjectively (e.g., "I didn't like this scene").
- Feedback must map exactly to a rule in the Constitution, Philosophy, or Specific Role Rubric.
- Must provide exact locations (Line/Page numbers) for all identified defects.

## 5. Performance & Quality

**KPIs:**
- False-positive error rate (flagging things that are actually correct).
- Escaped defect rate (errors that make it to publication).
- Turnaround time for manuscript evaluation.

**Quality Standards:**
- Must maintain 100% adherence to objective scoring rubrics.
- Zero tolerance for missing timeline contradictions.

## 6. Expansion Strategy

The Quality Division will scale by developing specialized evaluation roles, such as Sensitivity Readers, Legal Risk Assessors, and highly specific Genre Evaluators (e.g., Mystery Clue Auditors).

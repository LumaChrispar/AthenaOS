## 1. Scope

## 3. Core Concepts

### The Three-Act Structure (Athena Variant)
1. **Act I: The Setup (25%)**
   - Hook → Inciting Incident → Debate → Break into Act II.
   - Must establish the protagonist's baseline psychology (the "Lie They Believe").
2. **Act II: The Confrontation (50%)**
   - B-Story Introduction → Fun and Games (Rising Action) → Midpoint (False Victory/Defeat) → Bad Guys Close In → All Is Lost → Dark Night of the Soul.
   - The protagonist's internal flaw must be actively challenged by the external plot.
3. **Act III: The Resolution (25%)**
   - Break into Act III → Finale (Synthesis of internal growth and external action) → Final Image.

### Scene & Sequel Mechanics
- **Scene (Action):** Goal → Conflict → Disaster.
- **Sequel (Reaction):** Reaction → Dilemma → Decision (which forms the Goal of the next Scene).

## 4. Relationships
- **Tension vs. Pacing:** Tension is the *potential* energy of the conflict; Pacing is the *kinetic* energy (the speed at which information is revealed or stakes change).
- **Character Arc vs. Plot:** Plot is the external mechanism designed specifically to force the Character Arc.

# WF-001: Novel Production Pipeline

**Document ID:** WF-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## 1. Purpose
The end-to-end, linear pipeline for producing a novel within Athena Publishing OS. No stage may be skipped, and no stage may begin until the preceding stage has passed its Quality Assurance (QA) gate.

## 2. The Pipeline Stages

### Stage 1: Concept & Intake (CEO)
- **Action:** Analyze raw prompt/concept. Assess market viability.
- **Output:** Approved Project Brief.
- **Gate:** Strategic Approval (GOV-002).

### Stage 2: Architecture & Planning (Story Div. + Research Div.)
- **Action:** Literary Architect creates the structure. Character Psychologist profiles the cast. Research curates required knowledge.
- **Output:** The Project Bible (MEM-001).
- **Gate:** QA Logic Audit. (Does the plot make sense? Are characters consistent?)

### Stage 3: Drafting (Story Div.)
- **Action:** Literary Author drafts chapters sequentially.
- **Output:** Manuscript Draft (v1.0).
- **Gate:** QA Completeness Check. (Did the author follow the beat sheet exactly?)

### Stage 4: Developmental Edit (Production Div. + Story Div.)
- **Action:** Dev Editor critiques the draft. Story Div executes revisions.
- **Output:** Manuscript Draft (v2.0).
- **Gate:** QA Emotional & Structural Resonance Check.

### Stage 5: Copy Edit & Polish (Production Div.)
- **Action:** Copy Editor fixes grammar and syntax. Prose Polisher enhances style. Continuity Keeper does final timeline check.
- **Output:** Manuscript Draft (v3.0 - Final).
- **Gate:** QA Final Scorecard (Must meet minimum thresholds across all metrics).

### Stage 6: Production & Formatting (Production Div.)
- **Action:** Typesetting, metadata generation, blurb writing.
- **Output:** Publication-Ready Files (EPUB, PDF).
- **Gate:** CEO Final Sign-off.

### Stage 7: Post-Mortem (All Divs)
- **Action:** Analyze the performance of the pipeline. What failed? What was inefficient?
- **Output:** System Update Recommendations (SI-001).

## 3. Failure Handling (The Revision Loop)
If any Gate fails, the pipeline reverses. The QA Division generates a COM-001 report detailing the defects. The responsible Division must execute the requested changes and re-submit to the QA Gate. Repeated failures at the same gate trigger CEO escalation.

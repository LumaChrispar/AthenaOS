# QA-001: Chapter Review Checklist & Scoring

**Document ID:** QA-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## 1. Purpose
To provide the Literary Critic (EMP-009) and the QA Division with an objective, mathematically strict framework for evaluating individual chapters produced by the Story Division. Subjectivity is eliminated through strict criteria.

## 2. Scoring Methodology
Every chapter is scored out of 100 points, broken down into five categories worth 20 points each. A chapter must score a minimum of **85/100** to pass the QA gate. A score below 85 triggers an automatic Revision Loop (COM-001).

## 3. The Assessment Categories

### A. Architectural Adherence (20 points)
- [ ] Does the chapter begin and end exactly where the approved Beat Sheet dictated? (5 pts)
- [ ] Are all required narrative beats executed? (10 pts)
- [ ] Are there any unapproved deviations that break downstream logic? (5 pts)

### B. Tension & Pacing (20 points)
- [ ] Is there clear micro-tension or a micro-goal established within the first three paragraphs? (10 pts)
- [ ] Does the chapter end on a hook, a decision, or a revelation that compels the reader to continue? (10 pts)

### C. Character Consistency & Agency (20 points)
- [ ] Do the character's actions align perfectly with their psychological profile (EMP-003)? (10 pts)
- [ ] Are the plot advancements the result of active character choices, rather than coincidence? (10 pts)

### D. Continuity & World Logic (20 points)
- [ ] Is the timeline maintained perfectly from the previous chapter? (10 pts)
- [ ] Are the established rules of the world (physics, magic, society) strictly followed without exception? (10 pts)

### E. Prose Clarity & Subtext (20 points)
- [ ] Is the literal action happening in the scene immediately clear to the reader without re-reading? (10 pts)
- [ ] Does the dialogue contain appropriate subtext, avoiding "on-the-nose" exposition? (10 pts)

## 4. Defect Reporting
If a chapter fails, the QA agent must fill out a Defect Report (via COM-001), detailing exactly which points were deducted and why, citing specific line numbers. The QA agent may not rewrite the text; they may only identify the failure.

# PUB-001: Formatting and Production Standards

**Document ID:** PUB-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  

---

## 1. Purpose
To ensure that all outputs from Athena Publishing OS are technically flawless, beautifully formatted, and immediately ready for market distribution across digital and physical platforms without requiring human intervention in typesetting.

## 2. The Golden Master (Source of Truth)
Athena does not maintain separate word processing files for different formats. The ultimate source of truth for any completed book is a semantic, highly structured Markdown file. All output formats (EPUB, PDF, HTML) are compiled programmatically from this Golden Master using static site generators or publishing compilation tools (e.g., Pandoc).

## 3. Required Front and Back Matter
Every published file must autonomously generate and include:
- **Title Page:** Title, Subtitle, Author Name (or Athena OS Alias).
- **Copyright Page:** Year, ISBN, Edition, and the mandatory AI Disclosure Clause (per CONST-001: Ethics).
- **Table of Contents:** Dynamically generated with accurate anchoring/linking.
- **Dedication/Epigraph:** If specified in the Literary Architecture.
- **Author's Note / Afterword:** Generated by the CEO or Marketing Dept.

## 4. Typographical Standards
The Production Manager agent enforces the following strict rules during compilation:
- **Smart Quotes:** All straight quotes (`" '`) must be converted to typographically correct curly quotes (`“ ” ‘ ’`).
- **Dashes:** Em-dashes (`—`) must be used without spaces for interruptions or parenthetical thoughts. En-dashes (`–`) for ranges.
- **Scene Breaks:** Must use a standardized ornamental break (e.g., `* * *` or a custom SVG) rather than empty lines.
- **Paragraphs:** First paragraphs of chapters or after scene breaks must be flush left (no indent). Subsequent paragraphs must be indented. No block spacing between paragraphs in fiction.

## 5. Market Readiness Check
Before the final files are released by the Production Division, a deterministic script validates the EPUB against the official IDPF EpubCheck tool to ensure 100% compliance with digital storefront requirements (Amazon KDP, Apple Books, Kobo, etc.). Any failure halts the pipeline.

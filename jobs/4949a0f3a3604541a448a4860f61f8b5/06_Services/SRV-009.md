# SRV-009: Story Bible Manager

**Document ID:** SRV-009  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  
**Layer:** Service Layer  
**Type:** Permanent Core

---

## Identity & Mission
The custodian of `story_bible.json`. Owns every read and write operation on the project's single source of truth. No other service may write directly to the Story Bible — all updates must be routed through SRV-009.

## Responsibilities
- Execute SOP-004 after every `ChapterApproved` event.
- Parse `continuity_notes[]` from approved chapters and merge them into the Story Bible.
- Resolve conflicts between incoming continuity notes and existing Bible entries.
- Maintain the `unresolved_threads[]` list — the active questions the plot must answer.
- Expose Story Bible context fragments to other services on request.

## Failure Modes
- **Write Bypass:** Any service writing directly to `story_bible.json` without routing through SRV-009 is a critical violation. The Memory Auditor enforces this.
- **Stale Context:** Providing outdated Bible fragments to services causes continuity failures. Always serve the latest version.

## Interface Contract

```json
    "Error if called with UpdateStoryBible while a previous update is in-flight."
  ]
}
```

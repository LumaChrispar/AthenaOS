# SRV-014: Foreshadowing Specialist

**Document ID:** SRV-014  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  
**Layer:** Service Layer  
**Type:** Support

---

## Identity & Mission
The architect of inevitability. Plants narrative seeds in early chapters that bloom into major revelations later. A twist that feels cheap is unforeshadowed. A twist that feels inevitable is exquisitely foreshadowed. This service ensures the latter.

## Responsibilities
- Maintain a Foreshadowing Registry: a map of what is planted, where, and what it pays off.
- Review early chapters to ensure future major events have at least two planted seeds.
- Review late chapters to verify payoffs are earned by their seeds.
- Foreshadowing must be invisible on first read; blatant foreshadowing is a failure.

## Interface Contract

```json
{
  "service_id": "SRV-014",
  "capability": "Foreshadowing & Payoff Architecture",
  "inputs": [
    "outline.json (all major twists and revelations)",
    "chapter.schema.json (all drafted chapters)"
  ],
  "outputs": [
    "Foreshadowing Registry (seed-to-payoff map)",
    "ForeshadowingAuditComplete event"
  ],
  "dependencies": ["SRV-002", "SRV-005"],
  "failure_conditions": [
    "Error if any major plot revelation has zero foreshadowing seeds in prior chapters."
  ]
}
```

# SRV-025: Version Manager

**Document ID:** SRV-025  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  
**Layer:** Service Layer  
**Type:** Support

---

## Identity & Mission
The immutable ledger of the project. Maintains a complete, timestamped version history of every significant document — chapters, the Story Bible, the Outline, and schema objects. Ensures no approved work is ever permanently lost, even during aggressive revision cycles.

## Responsibilities
- Snapshot `story_bible.json`, `outline.json`, and all chapter files at every major state transition (e.g., `APPROVED` → `REVISION_REQUIRED`).
- Assign a version tag to every snapshot: `v[chapter].[revision]` (e.g., `CHAP-03.v2`).
- Maintain a full audit trail of every Defect Report and its corresponding revision.
- On request, restore any prior approved version of any document.

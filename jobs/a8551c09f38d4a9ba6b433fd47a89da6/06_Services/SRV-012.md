# SRV-012: Conflict Architect

**Document ID:** SRV-012  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  
**Layer:** Service Layer  
**Type:** Support

---

## Identity & Mission
Ensures that tension never drops to zero. Designs and manages three simultaneous layers of conflict across the manuscript: external (plot), internal (psychological), and interpersonal (relational). A scene without conflict is a scene that does not belong.

## Responsibilities
- Map the three conflict layers at each major story milestone.
- Identify scenes where all three conflict layers are absent and flag them as Dead Zones.
- Design escalation ladders: each conflict layer must intensify after its initial introduction.

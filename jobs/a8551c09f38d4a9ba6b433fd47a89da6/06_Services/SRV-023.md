# SRV-023: Originality Auditor

**Document ID:** SRV-023  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Locked  
**Layer:** Service Layer  
**Type:** Support

---

## Identity & Mission
Ensures the prose remains fresh across a long manuscript. Detects repetition — repeated phrases, sentence openings, chapter-ending patterns, and clichéd constructions — that degrade the reading experience over time.

## Responsibilities
- Scan all drafted prose for repeated phrases appearing more than 3 times across a manuscript.
- Detect chapters that share structural patterns with prior chapters (e.g., every chapter ending on a cliffhanger of the same type).
- Flag genre clichés that could be subverted or eliminated.
- Return an Originality Score from 0–10.

## Interface Contract

```json
{
  "service_id": "SRV-023",
  "capability": "Originality Analysis",
  "inputs": [
    "All chapter.schema.json prose_content (full manuscript context)",
    "config/quality.yaml (originality threshold)"
  ],
  "outputs": [
    "Originality Score (0-10)",
    "Repetition Report (flagged phrases + chapter references)",
    "OriginalityCheckComplete event"
  ],
  "dependencies": ["SRV-010 (dispatches this service)"],
  "failure_conditions": [
    "Error if fewer than 2 chapters are available (cannot detect cross-chapter repetition with 1 chapter).",
    "Score reduced by 1 for every phrase repeated more than 5 times."
  ]
}
```

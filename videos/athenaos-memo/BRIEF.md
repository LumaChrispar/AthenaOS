## Intent

Show off AthenaOS, the user's 30-service novel-writing operating system, as a
classified internal memo about *their* novel. The user chose the "Internal memo"
concept from the pitch round: the video is formatted as a redacted government-
style document, and redaction bars slide off to reveal the real pipeline behind
them. Tone: procedural, dry, authoritative — the rigor is the message, no
hype, no cinematic montage. Mostly kinetic typography, which is what the
framework renders best.

## Assets

- None supplied. Every visual is invented (faceless explainer).

## Customizations

- Classified-document framing: memo header block, field labels, redaction bars
  that wipe to reveal content.
- Kinetic typography carries the piece; diagrams second it.

## Notes

- Real material from the repo, not invented: 30 services (SRV-001..SRV-030),
  five phases (Architecture / Character+World / Voice Calibration / Drafting /
  Editing / Publishing), QA gates, governance layer, SRV-021 reserved.
- No stock-photo or "AI magic" aesthetics. Avoid the generic glowing-robot
  explainer look entirely.
- Silent-friendly: no user-supplied script, so narration is TTS.

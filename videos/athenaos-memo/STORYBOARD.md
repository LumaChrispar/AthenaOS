## Video direction

**Palette system** (from `frame.md`, code-editorial — never invented): `cream` is the document surface and base plane; `ink` is all type and every rule; `tile` / `tile-strong` are the memo field bands and the redaction bar fill; `coral` is the single scarce voltage — the `CLASSIFIED` stamp, the count-up number, the `PASS` marks, and the redaction bar's leading edge only. Nothing else is colored.

**Motion grammar + shot model.** Long-tail `power3` easing throughout — nothing bouncy; this is a document, not a toy. The reveal model is **VO-paced**: at t=0 only what the voice names then is on screen; each further element arrives on its spoken cue, with the back ~50% of every frame still delivering. The signature move of the whole video is the **redaction wipe**: a solid `tile-strong` bar sits over a line, then a clip-path wipe clears it left-to-right with a thin coral leading edge, revealing the text underneath. It is deliberately the same gesture as the `wipe` seam, so the transition and the theme are one idea.

**Rhythm / held-frame allocation.** Frame 5 (voice fingerprint) is the **held breather** — its block completes early and reads still for the back third. Frame 6 (the gate) is the second, quieter hold: the `PASS` stamps settle and the shot stays put. Everything else reveals to the VO. Frame 7 is the only frame with a real exit.

**Framings — at least three, never the same twice in a row:** full-width strip (F1, F3) · rule-of-thirds with a right-margin rail (F2, F5) · centered (F4) · full-width strip, mirrored (F6) · centered cover block (F7).

**Type.** Everything is set from `frame.md`'s ramp by role — `kicker` / `mono-label` for the memo field labels (uppercase, tracked), `headline` / `display-italic` for the hero values in Garamond, `code` for service numbers and status marks. Type is the primary visual here; the hero element always fills 40–60% of frame.

**Caption band.** All content is planned into the top ~83%; a centered hero anchors at y ≈ 0.42 × height.

**Negative list.** No icons. No illustration or iconography standing in for a metaphor. No purple-blue "AI" gradients, no glowing orbs, no bokeh, no lens flares. No browser chrome or UI mock — this is a document, and the topic is the document. Never slideshow (front-load then freeze) and never screensaver (everything drifting independently): reveals are cued to the spoken line, and a resolved frame holds still. No motion after resolution except a subtle jitter.

# AthenaOS — Source Notes for Explainer Video

Working title: THE MANUSCRIPT FILE — internal memo, classified.
Subject: one novel, moving through a governed pipeline.

## What AthenaOS is

AthenaOS is a custom publishing operating system for novel writing. It is not a
prompt. It is not a chatbot with a system message. It is a 30-service pipeline
where every stage of turning an idea into a finished, edited, publishable novel
is a separate service with its own written protocol, its own inputs, its own
outputs, and its own quality gate.

Real structure from the repository:

- 30 services, numbered SRV-001 through SRV-030. (SRV-021 is reserved — a
  deliberate gap in the numbering, not a missing file.)
- Each service is a written .md protocol. A service is dispatched as a subagent
  seeded with its full unabridged protocol file as persona.
- 16 top-level directories: Constitution, Philosophy, Governance, Registry,
  Organization, Departments, Services, Domains, SOPs, Memory, Communication,
  Workflows, QualityAssurance, Automation, SelfImprovement, Publishing.

## The five phases

1. ARCHITECTURE — SRV-002, the Literary Architect. Decides the shape of the book
   before a word is written: structure, act breaks, POV strategy, target length.
2. CHARACTER & WORLD — SRV-003 Character Psychologist, SRV-004 World Builder.
   Interiority and world systems, built before drafting.
3. VOICE CALIBRATION — SRV-028, the Voice Calibrator. Produces a 500-word voice
   fingerprint. Runs after world building, before drafting. SRV-027 is the
   Voice Variation Engine, which de-AI-ifies the prose.
4. DRAFTING — SRV-005 Literary Author, SRV-006 Shakespearean Stylist.
5. EDITING — SRV-007 Developmental Editor through SRV-016. Sixteen services of
   developmental editing, line editing, and structural passes.

PUBLISHING — SRV-017 through SRV-026 takes it to market.

Quality services sit across all of it: SRV-029 Opening Specialist (three
strategic alternate openings after Chapter 1 QA), SRV-030 Reader Proxy (a naive
reader audit — does the prose actually land on someone who hasn't been briefed).

## The two things that make it different

**Governance before generation.** There is a Constitution, a Governance layer,
and a Registry that exist before any service runs. The rules of the system are
written down and enforced, not implied by whatever prompt happened to run.

**Voice is measured, not vibes.** A 500-word voice fingerprint is taken before
drafting starts, and SRV-027 exists specifically to strip AI tells out of the
prose afterward. The pipeline treats prose quality as something with a spec and
a test.

## Audience and tone

For the owner of the system, and for anyone who has tried to get a model to
write a whole novel and watched it flatline in chapter four. Dry, precise,
documentary. The memo format does the persuading: this reads like an internal
record of a process that is already running.

## Core message

AthenaOS is not a prompt that writes your novel. It is a governed pipeline that
does — thirty services, five phases, and a quality gate at every seam.
